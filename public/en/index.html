Since 2021 we have won the eAwards México and the ILAN Award, which took us to Israel; we presented in Colombia, were mentored by Simón Cohen of Shark Tank México, and this year Alan Soubran, our CEO, received the State Youth Award for innovation.

Alba Space. Perception beyond.

A 3-in-1 instrument for small satellites: a single camera that finds its way by the stars, detects space debris and guides docking with another vehicle.

  • Navigate by the stars

    It photographs the sky, recognizes the stars and calculates where the satellite is pointing, knowing nothing beforehand.

  • Detect space debris

    It compares consecutive photos, isolates what moves among the stars and reports its trajectory.

  • Approach and dock

    Extending its baffle changes the focal length, switching from the whole sky to a close view of the target.
