UNBLIND. We see with the heart.

AI glasses for people with visual disability. They read, describe, recognize and guide you with 3D audio, and you use them with your voice.

In development · not yet for sale
Listen with headphones What it does
It isn't about seeing with your eyes. It's about perceiving the world differently.

Hear where things are.

UNBLIND's 3D audio makes every person and object sound from where they are: to your left, in front or behind. And with LiDAR, the same sound tells you where obstacles are.

Put on headphones and hear how it sounds.

What it does, seen the UNBLIND way.

The same street, told five ways. Tap one or let it change.

It also reads text aloud and recognizes products and codes. While it is in development, it does not replace the white cane or the guide dog.

Light, accessible and with AI inside.

A prototype built for everyday use: a camera, LiDAR, a processor and bone-conduction sound, in a pair of light glasses. Designed and tested with blind and low-vision people.
