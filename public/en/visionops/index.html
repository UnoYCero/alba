Data analytics with computer vision. We are starting with restaurants and food businesses, without facial recognition.

Your cameras already see your business. We turn it into data.

Vision Ops is the data analytics of Alba Vision: it connects the cameras you already have and turns what happens in your operation into times, zones, findings and decisions.

In operation · we start with restaurants and food businesses

Scroll to see how it works

  1. What gets detectedPeople, loads or parts: everything that moves in front of the camera becomes an anonymous dot. Never a face.
  2. The pathsVision Ops follows each dot while it is in frame and rebuilds where it went.
  3. Zones and their timesThe floor is split into zones: how long people wait, how long things take and how much each one is used.
  4. The patternsDay after day, the peak hours and the places where everything piles up show up.
  5. The findingWhat matters is flagged on its own, with the frame that backs it up.
  6. The dashboardIt all arrives in a clear report, and you can ask it questions in your own words.

Example

We count what happens. We recognize no one.

  • No facial recognitionIt detects silhouettes and follows them while they are in frame. It does not know who anyone is.
  • Encrypted and deletedThe video travels encrypted and the original is deleted on its own after 30 days.
  • Only the evidenceThe moment of each finding is kept, and deleted with the rest.
  • The connector is yoursIt reads only the video you authorize and you can remove it whenever you want.
