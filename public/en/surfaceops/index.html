The office receives
something to work with.

A dashboard with a map, filters and a record for each pothole. Photo, location, measurements and work stages in one place.

PDFAutoCAD · DXFExcel

Calculated volume and materials depend on the recorded depth. AI estimation is not a field-validated measurement.

The street does not wait
for the signal to return.

  1. Capture and measure.

    Record the pothole offline. To use AI offline, download the models to the phone beforehand.

  2. It stays on the phone.

    Information is stored locally while there is no connection.

  3. The signal returns; it uploads automatically.

    Once the connection returns, records sync so the office can receive them.

For those who work
on the street.

  • Municipalities

    Review locations and organize records coming in from the field.

  • Construction companies

    Bring measurements and drawings into pothole repair work.

  • Site supervision

    Review photos, measurements and stages recorded by the team.

These are proposed uses. Surface Ops is in testing, with no pilots or customers.
