What Órbita does.

  • Organizes conversations

    And lets someone on your team review and serve the customer.

  • Checks your catalog

    It uses the catalog and prices your business set up.

  • Prepares quotes

    And registers the request only when the customer explicitly confirms.

  • Keeps track

    It keeps references to check the status and avoid duplicate requests.

Your team always decides.

A registered request requires human review: it does not confirm a payment, a delivery or a kitchen order on its own. Your team keeps the responsibility for approving orders and can step in to serve the customer whenever needed.

WhatsApp and your data.

Privacy notice (in Spanish) Delete your data (in Spanish)

The connection uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform interfaces, when the business authorizes it and completes its registration with Meta.

Messages are encrypted on the server before being stored, and the data is not used for advertising or sold.
